# Scene 1: Backstory (The Origin)
- **Setting**: Gibbs tells Detective Gray how he became a consultant while in the BPD Breakroom.
- **Flashback**:
    - Gibbs breaks into a factory at the docks to sleep (omits the breaking in part to Gray)
    - Finds a corpse, faints from panic, and sleepwalks.        
    - Comes up with detailed analysis via three clues: empty drink moved to table center, shredded wiretap, neatly pushed-in chair.
    - BPD cops barge in; hire him as a "genius consultant" after a week of clearance.
- **Present**: Gray reacts with sarcasm; Gibbs leaves her to her break.
## Breakroom Exploration
- **Setting**: BPD Breakroom

- **Exit**: Interacting with the door triggers Captain Bolton.
